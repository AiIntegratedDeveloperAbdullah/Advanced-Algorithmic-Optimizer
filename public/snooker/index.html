How to play

Controls & rules

Aim
Drag on the table, or ← → (Shift for fine). In overhead view, drag toward where you want to aim.
Stance
Drag up/down, wheel, or ↑ ↓
Strike
Drag the power bar down and release. Or hold Space, release to hit, Esc to cancel.
Spin
Top of the cue ball for follow, bottom for screw, sides for side. W A S D nudge, C centres.
Ball on
Red, then any colour, alternating. Once the reds are gone: yellow, green, brown, blue, pink, black.
Fouls
Wrong ball first, missing everything, potting the wrong ball, or going in-off. Opponent gets 4 or the value of the ball involved, up to 7.
